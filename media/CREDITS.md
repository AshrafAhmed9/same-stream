## Disclosed limitation
`coimbra-mondego` and `benevento-calore-lowflow` show larger river reaches,
not small urban streams of the exact scale OAH's citizen protocol targets
(a 100m reach). They're included for city coverage and because a wider
channel is still a legitimate real-world case the app's questions must
handle. This is noted in `PREREGISTRATION.md` as a scope limitation, not
hidden.

## Reference/urban imagery (guided-flow extras)
Images referenced from `public/reference-photos/urban/` (gabion mesh, small
urban weir, concrete trapezoid channel, vegetation height scale, orientation
diagram) are original diagrams/photos prepared for this project — see that
folder's own credits file once populated.

## OAH app's own reference images
`public/reference-photos/oah-app/1.png` through `20.png`/`.jpg` are mirrored
directly from the live OneAquaHealth Citizen Science App
(`apps.oneaquahealth.eu/citizen/questionnaire-images/`) for the baseline
replica, which must show citizens exactly what the real app shows. These are
OneAquaHealth/ENORA's own illustrations, used here solely to build a
faithful, verifiable baseline for a study about their app — not
redistributed as a standalone asset.
